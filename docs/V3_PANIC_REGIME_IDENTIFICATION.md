# Gate V3-3A — Panic-Consistent Regime Identification Freeze

## Status

> `IDENTIFICATION_CONTRACT_FROZEN`

This subgate freezes how the Version 3 panic-consistent latent regime may be identified. It does not claim that the probability engine has been implemented, empirically validated, or finally locked.

## Scientific estimand

The engine will estimate

\[
p_t
=
P\!\left(
R_t=\mathrm{PANIC\_CONSISTENT\_REGIME}
\mid
\mathcal{F}_t
\right),
\]

where \(R_t\) is latent and \(\mathcal{F}_t\) contains only evidence measurable at or before \(t\).

The probability means that current market evidence is consistent with a broad panic-transmission mechanism under the declared model. It is not a direct observation of investor psychology, a deterministic crisis label, or a forecast that a crash must occur.

## Identification boundary

No approved external panic label exists. Therefore:

1. supervised panic estimation is prohibited;
2. the monotone score is a governed mechanism-consistency probability, not an externally calibrated event frequency;
3. the HMM output is a forward-filtered posterior under the declared latent-state model;
4. the transparent Version 2 state model remains a challenger and cannot produce a panic probability;
5. no registered model may be selected, promoted, ranked, or silently combined.

## Mechanism system

The information set is

\[
X_t
=
\left[
S_t,
N_t,
L_t,
F_t,
V_t,
D_t
\right],
\]

covering spectral structure, network topology, liquidity, funding and leverage, volatility, and downside dynamics.

A valid panic-consistent probability requires at least four of the six families and evidence from all three blocks:

- structural evidence: spectral or network;
- market-plumbing evidence: liquidity or funding;
- price-risk evidence: volatility or downside.

Otherwise the published state is

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{INSUFFICIENT\_MECHANISM\_EVIDENCE}}.
\]

This prevents volatility, drawdown, funding stress, or any other single mechanism from being relabelled as panic.

## Causal historical scaling

Each feature is standardized against strictly prior observations:

\[
z_{j,t}
=
d_j
\frac{x_{j,t}-\operatorname{median}(x_{j,<t})}
{1.4826\,\operatorname{MAD}(x_{j,<t})+\varepsilon},
\]

where \(d_j\) is the predeclared panic direction.

Appending future data cannot revise an earlier standardized feature, mechanism score, probability, state, interval, or deterministic row identity.

## Registered models

### Transparent Version 2 state challenger

The existing range, trend, and stress layer remains visible as a transparent challenger. It cannot claim panic and cannot alter any Version 2 result.

### Monotone mechanism score

For available and sufficient mechanism families,

\[
q_t=\sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}_t}w_{g,t}M_g(t),
\qquad
p_t=\frac{1}{1+\exp\{-6(q_t-0.5)\}}.
\]

Weights are equal and predeclared. No label fitting or automatic weight optimization is allowed.

### Causal Gaussian HMM

The HMM has two latent states and consumes the six mechanism-family scores. Parameters are fitted only on expanding historical prefixes. The published probability is the forward-filtered probability of the state anchored as panic-consistent.

Full-sample retrospective smoothing is prohibited.

## Label-switching control

The panic-consistent state must have:

1. the highest mean composite mechanism pressure; and
2. directionally higher pressure in at least four of six mechanism families.

The permutation map and anchor statistics must be persisted. A failed anchor invalidates the model rather than permitting convenient relabelling.

## Uncertainty

Every valid probability must include a 95% interval generated by a deterministic, prefix-respecting moving-block bootstrap. A point estimate without a valid interval is not publishable.

## Operational interpretation

The central interpretation policy is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
0.00 \le p_t < 0.25
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{LOW\_PANIC\_CONSISTENCY},\\
0.25 \le p_t < 0.50
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{STRESS\_BUILDING},\\
0.50 \le p_t < 0.75
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{TRANSMISSION\_ESCALATION},\\
0.75 \le p_t \le 1.00
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{PANIC\_CONSISTENT\_REGIME}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Threshold sensitivity is mandatory. Thresholds cannot change the underlying probability or select a preferred model.

## Occupancy and duration
